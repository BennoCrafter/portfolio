Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. TrackStar does not collect, process, store, or share any data about its users. Everything the app saves, like your datasets and the players of a game, stays on your device.

To play songs, TrackStar uses Apple Music on your device. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The camera is only used to scan the QR codes on the cards; no images are saved or sent anywhere.

Datasets are downloaded from our public GitHub repository. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address for these downloads, under GitHub's privacy statement.

If you have questions about our privacy policy, need assistance, or wish to report any privacy-related concerns, please contact us via email at timetablemanager.contact@gmail.com.

By reaching out to us, you may provide us with personal information, such as your email address or any details included in your inquiry. This information will be used solely to respond to your query and will not be shared with third parties.
