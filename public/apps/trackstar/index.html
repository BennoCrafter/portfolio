Side A · Party

One phone, passed around the room. Turn it sideways: it plays the songs and keeps everyone’s timeline.

  • On your turn the phone plays a song and you place it in your timeline.
  • Everyone else gets 5 seconds to challenge: spend a star and pick a different gap.
  • Name the song to earn a star, up to 5. Three stars buy a card that slots in for free.
  • Right spot: you keep the card. Wrong, but a challenger got it right: they steal it.
  • Pick a level per player. Rookies see the era’s color as a hint.
A2 Quick DrawA buzzer race, one snippet at a time. 3–6 players≈ 20 min
2s8s20s30s
  • The song plays in snippets: 2, 8, 20, then 30 seconds.
  • Buzz in and name it out of four. The earlier you buzz, the more points (4, 3, 2, 1).
  • Wrong guess? You’re locked out for that song and the music keeps going.
  • Nobody buzzes? The player with the fewest cards places it blind.
  • First to 15 points wins.

Side B · Solo & cards

B1 Higher or LowerSolo. How long can you go? 1 player3–10 min

EarlierLater
  • Is the next song older or newer than the one on the table? Keep the streak going.
  • Three lives. Every 5th song is golden: guess its decade for an extra one.
  • Daily Mix: the same 10 songs for everyone, once a day per pack. Share your result.
  • Leaderboards for your longest streak and the Daily Mix.
B2 Card GamePrinted cards on the table. Any groupOpen end
  • Play with real cards, like the board game. The phone only scans the QR code and plays the song.
  • Print your own from any song pack or Apple Music playlist, or use the original German Hitster cards.
  • Pick the rules before the first scan: Classic, Jam Session or Year Bet.
  • Optional star counter on screen. Rookies can peek at the era’s color.
