TrackStar

Guess the release year. Build the timeline.
An open-source, Hitster-inspired music game for iPhone.

Download on theApp Store
▶ GitHub

Side A · With cards

You play with real cards on the table, like the board game. Print your own from the app (any song pack or Apple Music playlist becomes a PDF: QR codes on the front, artist, year and title on the back) or use the original German Hitster cards. The phone only scans and plays the song.

1. Scan

Scan the QR code on a card. TrackStar plays the song through Apple Music. No peeking at the back.

2. Place

Put the card in your timeline, back side down: before, after or between the cards already there.

3. Flip

Turn it over and check the year. Right spot? The card stays. Wrong? It's gone. Then it's the next player's turn.

4. Win

The first player with 10 cards in their timeline wins.
