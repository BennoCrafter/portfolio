TrackStar

Guess the release year. Build the timeline.
An open-source, Hitster-inspired music game for iPhone.

▶ GitHub ▶ Discord

Side A · Two ways to play

With Cards

Scan a printed TrackStar card or an original German Hitster card. The song plays, you place it in your timeline, then flip the card to check.

No Cards

Nothing to print. Turn the phone sideways: it picks the songs and keeps everyone's timeline. Whoever recognizes a song places it: +1 if it fits, −1 if not. First to 10 wins.

Side B · Features

  1. B1Works with the original Hitster cards
  2. B2Make decks from Apple Music playlists
  3. B3Print your own cards from the app
  4. B4Fully open source
